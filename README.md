# 🔮 MindSpell

A mathematical spell disguised as magic.

> *Conjure a number… silently.*

MindSpell is a magical, chaotic, interactive experience where mathematics meets witchcraft.

Two witches are in the middle of their mystical ritual when their crystal balls are stolen by mischievous cockroaches. The thieves escape in a UFO while an angry Oggy chases after them.

And somehow, in the middle of all this chaos, the witches still have time for a little magic. 🔮✨

## 🧙‍♀️ The Story

The witches' crystal balls are stolen by a group of cockroaches who make their escape in a UFO.

As Oggy runs after them, the witches begin their own spell.

The user is invited to think of a number and follow a series of mathematical instructions cast by the witch.

The user performs each calculation themselves, keeping their original number hidden.

At the end of the spell, MindSpell determines the result produced by those instructions — making a simple mathematical trick feel like something much more mysterious.

## ✨ The Experience

The website is designed as a short magical journey:

1. 🔮 Enter the world of MindSpell.
2. 🧙‍♀️ Watch the witches begin their ritual.
3. 🪳 The cockroaches steal the crystal balls.
4. 🛸 They escape in a UFO while Oggy chases them.
5. 💥 The crystal-ball chaos leads into the spell.
6. 🪄 The witch casts a series of mathematical instructions.
7. 🔢 Think of a number and follow the instructions.
8. ✨ Enter the resulting number.
9. 🔮 MindSpell reveals what the spell has produced.

## 🎭 What Makes It Different

MindSpell isn't meant to be a traditional game, calculator, or productivity tool.

It's a small interactive magic trick built around a simple idea:

*Can mathematics be made to feel like magic?*

The experience combines storytelling, animation, video, interaction, and a mathematical trick to create a moment of:

> *"Wait... HOW did it know that?"*
